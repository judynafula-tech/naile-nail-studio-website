NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your appointment.

Do you offer house-call appointments?

Yes! You can request a house-call appointment through our booking form. Enter your area or estate so we can check availability.

Can I visit the nail studio instead?

Yes! Select "In-Person Appointment" when completing the booking form. The studio location will be shared when your appointment is confirmed.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form with your preferred service, appointment type, date and time. Your request will open in WhatsApp so you can send it.

Is my appointment confirmed immediately?

No. Submitting a booking request does not automatically confirm your appointment. We will contact you through WhatsApp to confirm availability.

Can I bring my own nail design inspiration?

Absolutely! Describe your preferred design in the booking form. You can also share reference pictures through WhatsApp.

Are the prices different for house calls?

No. The listed service prices are the same whether you choose a house-call appointment or an In-Person Appointment.

What if I cannot explain my house-call location?

You can contact us by phone and explain your location directly.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

If you need to change or cancel your appointment, please contact us through WhatsApp as early as possible.
